### Checks required

1. Identify the exact GHL screen, field name, underlying record type, and account/location containing the value. Preserve its source reference and observation date.
2. Establish whether it represents a product/offer price, recurring subscription charge, issued invoice, actual payment, outstanding balance, or some other value. Do not assume these are interchangeable.
3. Establish whose financial relationship it describes: a WIA client paying WIA, or an end customer paying a WIA client inside that client's sub-account.
4. Confirm the linked client, offer, currency, billing interval, relevant dates, and payment status where applicable.
5. Compare a representative record with Dubsado and its Gmail notifications. Determine whether it is the same transaction, a separate recurring charge, a migrated offer, or unrelated data.
6. Confirm which offers currently use Dubsado versus GHL and which system maintains the current invoice/payment state. Avoid counting the same payment twice across systems.
7. Inspect actual Dubsado email samples to establish which identifiers, dates, amounts, statuses and URLs can be extracted and whether linked documents are readable by the ingestion account. A notification may contain less information than its linked record.

### Why verification is necessary

On 2 September at 8:45, Gisselle described checking overdue invoices in GHL. At 23:38, Whitney described Dubsado generating the agreement and first invoice and sending signing/payment alerts to Gmail. At 24:09, she described moving offers to GHL, with one offer already migrated. These statements establish a historically mixed workflow, not the meaning of the specific pricing attribute seen now.

**Closure evidence:** An identified GHL field/record, a matched Dubsado/email example where applicable, confirmed ownership and financial meaning, and a documented source rule by offer/record type.

**Resulting decision:** Keep Dubsado-based event intake as directed. Add or retain GHL financial intake only for the verified information it contributes. Until verified, do not present the observed GHL price as a contract value, payment, debt, or client revenue.
