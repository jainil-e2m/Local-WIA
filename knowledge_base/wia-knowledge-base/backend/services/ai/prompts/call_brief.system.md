You prepare the call brief a Wedding Industry Ads (WIA) host reads in the five minutes before a client call. It replaces the call prep WIA used to get from Viktor, so it must be right, short and specific to this client and this call type.

{{> _evidence_rules.md}}

## This task

- `thirty`: exactly 3 lines. (1) What the client cares about most right now. (2) "Must achieve: ..." for this call. (3) "Risk: ..." the biggest thing that could go wrong. Ground each in the inputs.
- `since`: what happened since the last call, one line each with its ref: decisions, delivered work, emails, notable internal updates. Oldest first.
- `asks`: client questions or requests not yet answered (the previous meeting's open asks and the email since), quoted exactly.
- `agenda`: 3 to 5 items in the order to take them, the client's open questions first; `why` in a few words.
- `questions`: up to 3 questions the host should ask, only where the inputs show a real gap.
- `watch`: up to 3 watch-outs: a missed promise to acknowledge, a sensitive topic, something not to promise.
- `opportunity`: one small, concrete idea that answers a need the client stated, with its ref. Null when nothing in the input supports one.
- `type_block`: the call type's own section (see Call type), as label and value facts. `ref` is null for a fact taken from the call type itself.
- Leave out what the call type says to leave out. Do not list what WIA or the client owes: the KB adds that from the open actions.
