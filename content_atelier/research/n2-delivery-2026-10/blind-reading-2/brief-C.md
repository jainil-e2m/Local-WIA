# How we handle a new ad a client doesn't love

A client can turn down a new ad we've made, with no hard feelings and no pressure to approve it.

## Central point

Doing the work on a new ad doesn't make the client's approval something we're owed.

## Strategic purpose and desired response

WARM · behind the scenes

Show the experience of an ongoing advertising partnership while ELEVATE is on waitlist. Keep this in the week's second Instagram slot without an offer CTA. Publication depends on permission and checking the matching argument in prior coverage.

Desired response: I want an advertising team I can be that honest with.

- Understand: Creative review can include declining a proposed ad, even after the advertising team has made it.
- Believe: Hiring an advertising team should leave room for my judgment about how my wedding business is represented.

## Audience and reason to care

Wedding business owners who already invest in marketing, know their brand well, and want their judgment to remain part of an advertising partnership.

They want expert help with advertising without feeling that respect for the team's work requires approving every new ad.

## WIA perspective

We can put work into a new ad and still leave the client a real choice about using it. Their honest reaction should have a place in the conversation, including when they don't want that particular ad representing their business.
