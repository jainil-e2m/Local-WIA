# The time between booking a venue tour and arriving deserves a plan

When a couple schedules a tour a few weeks away, there's an opportunity to help them picture their wedding before they arrive. We look at what happens during that wait, including the emails they receive and where a personal conversation would help.

## WIA perspective

When a couple books a venue tour a few weeks away, we look at what they receive while they wait and where a personal conversation could help. There's an opportunity to show them more of the wedding they could have there, before they arrive.

We recommend giving that couple something useful to explore: wedding footage, an invitation to ask questions or an earlier phone or virtual connection with the owner. Confirmation and reminders handle the practical details. The rest of the follow-up can help the couple become more familiar with the venue and the people behind it.

That's how we approach nurture. Automated emails and texts support the owner's sales conversation and create opportunities for personal connection.

## Supporting example

In a conversation about venue follow-up, our team proposed taking a closer look at the language in the messages between inquiry and tour, with particular attention to making them more personal.

What WIA did: Whitney suggested offering an earlier phone or virtual connection. Our team also proposed a supplemental email with wedding footage and an invitation to ask questions for couples with a longer wait before their visit.

What the client contributed: The discussion brought the tour calendar into our thinking: what could a couple experience before arriving, even when the in-person visit stays on its scheduled date?
