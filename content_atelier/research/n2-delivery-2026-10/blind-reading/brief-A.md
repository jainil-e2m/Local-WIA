# How we decide whether to replace a client's existing page

We make a page we've built earn its place against the client's pages that already work, rather than moving all their ad traffic just because it's our page.

## Central point

Our work should earn its place in a client's advertising, with the same scrutiny we give the work already there.

## Strategic purpose and desired response

WARM · process or framework

Build trust during the ELEVATE waitlist window by showing the judgment behind a page-replacement decision, without turning the Instagram assets into offer posts.

Desired response: I want a team that respects what's already working, even when they built the alternative.

- Understand: Building a new landing page and deciding to replace existing ad destinations are separate decisions.
- Believe: WIA is willing to evaluate and refine its own work rather than give it an automatic preference.
- Do: Reply to the email to start a conversation about the right next step for their advertising.

## Audience and reason to care

Wedding business owners already investing in marketing and ads who want to expand their reach with a thoughtful advertising partner.

They want new expertise to build on their existing investment, with clear reasoning behind decisions that change where couples land.

## WIA perspective

When we build a landing page, we're responsible for more than delivering something new. We also have to decide whether it deserves to replace the pages already in your advertising. Our work belongs in that evaluation too.
