We want enough evidence to make a useful recommendation. In office hours, Carmen considered how much traffic a venue campaign had received, looked at what visitors were doing after the click and recommended Jacqueline's feedback on the landing page. She kept the open questions open.

Sharper point (proposed): Giving a campaign more time can go alongside a landing-page review. Expert guidance gives the owner something useful to examine while the campaign gathers more data.
Why: Makes the practical value of Carmen's judgment and collaboration clear, without turning the piece into another general lesson about tracking.

## WIA perspective

When we recommend giving a campaign more time, we also look at what would be useful to examine along the way. Our Google Ads lead considers how much traffic there is to assess and what visitors do after the click. In her work, visits lasting only a few seconds signal that something's off with the ads. Visitors who spend time exploring and then stop short of an inquiry prompt a closer look at the page. Those observations guide the next question. Bringing in our Meta ads specialist for landing-page feedback gives the owner another way to examine the experience while the campaign gathers more data.
