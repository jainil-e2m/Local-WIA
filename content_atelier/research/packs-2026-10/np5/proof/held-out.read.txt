She asked whether I wanted her to describe what she would love to see or what actually happens.

I told her I meant something closer to what actually happens, like what a current couple is experiencing.

**A venue's direction and a current couple's day can be understood without being merged into one story.**

If you're developing the next chapter of your venue, you may have a detailed picture of the weddings you want to host in the future. That picture can be meaningful alongside the experience of couples there now.

One account describes where you're taking the business. The other describes what a couple currently experiences. Both can have a place in the discussion, while remaining different accounts.

In this exchange, the owner named that distinction and I clarified the scope of the walkthrough. There was no need to make her vision part of the present-day description for it to matter in the conversation.

Whoever helps with your marketing should be curious about where your venue is going, and precise about which wedding day they're hoping to hear about when they ask.

Talk soon,
