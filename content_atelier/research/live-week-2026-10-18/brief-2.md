# Why Carmen wanted more time and another set of eyes on a venue campaign

We want enough evidence to make a useful recommendation. In office hours, Carmen considered how much traffic a venue campaign had received, looked at what visitors were doing after the click and recommended Jacqueline's feedback on the landing page. She kept the open questions open.

## WIA perspective

In a venue campaign review, Carmen held two things together: the campaign needed more breathing room, and she recommended seeking another set of eyes on the landing page. She considered the traffic available, looked at behavior after the click and recommended asking Jacqueline for feedback. She also said she hadn't reached a conclusion. That's what we want our guidance to do: help an owner decide what to examine next while being clear about what the evidence can tell us. Good marketing decisions start with good data, and part of that work is knowing when a question is still open.

## Supporting example

Carmen was reviewing a venue's corporate-events campaign. She said it hadn't had enough breathing room to assess success. Later, she described a visitor going straight to the inquiry form and then moving away from the page. These are observations reported during the conversation, not independently verified visitor data. She recommended submitting the corporate landing page to Jacqueline for feedback and explicitly said she wasn't reaching a conclusion. Credit Carmen for that recommendation. The evidence does not show Jacqueline speaking, reviewing the page or providing feedback.

What the client contributed: The venue's corporate campaign and landing page provided the account-specific material under review. The supplied excerpts do not establish an owner response, subsequent changes, membership status or who managed the campaign, so none should be invented.

## Intended shift

- Understand: Traffic volume and behavior after the click offer different information. Neither established the cause in this scene.
- Believe: Expert guidance can be valuable before there is a settled answer, especially when it identifies a focused next review. Carmen's recommendation to seek Jacqueline's feedback illustrates a proposed next step, not a contribution Jacqueline made in this conversation.
- Do: When reviewing a campaign, distinguish what has been observed from what still needs investigating before deciding what to change.
