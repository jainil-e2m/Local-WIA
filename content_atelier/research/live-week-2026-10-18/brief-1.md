# The time between booking a venue tour and arriving deserves a plan

When a couple schedules a tour a few weeks away, there's an opportunity to help them picture their wedding before they arrive. We look at what happens during that wait, including the emails they receive and where a personal conversation would help.

## WIA perspective

We were looking at what a venue's couples received between booking a tour and arriving for it. Whitney suggested an earlier phone or virtual connection for couples whose visit was weeks away, and our team suggested a supplemental email with wedding footage and an invitation to ask questions. Both were recommendations on that call, not changes we have documented. They reflect how we think about follow-up: automated emails and texts support the owner's own sales conversation and give couples something useful to engage with while they wait.
