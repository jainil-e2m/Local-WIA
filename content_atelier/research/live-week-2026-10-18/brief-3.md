# I want to know what made a couple book, as well as what they loved afterward

I want to understand what a couple was weighing when they chose your business. Their review can tell us what they loved about the experience. Asking about the booking decision can give us different information to consider when we write your ads.

## WIA perspective

In an internal team conversation about our own marketing, I brought up a question we ask new ELEVATE clients: do they have any research about why couples chose them? Reviews are useful evidence of the experience a business delivers. I also want to understand the decision that came before that experience. What was the couple weighing, and what helped them feel ready to book? I proposed asking newly booked couples about that decision and considering their answers when we write ad copy and landing pages. That remains an idea to explore. The principle is the part worth sharing: understanding what matters to your couples gives us more to work with when we communicate why they should choose you.

## Audience

Wedding business owners with strong reviews and an established marketing presence who want to reach more best-fit couples through advertising.

Their reviews already describe a valuable experience. They may reasonably wonder what else an ads team needs to know. This piece explains why understanding the booking decision adds another useful perspective.

## Intended shift

- Understand: What couples loved after their wedding and what mattered while they were choosing a vendor are different questions. Both can inform how a business communicates its value.
- Believe: WIA's advertising work involves understanding the reasons couples choose a business, as well as representing the experience it delivers.
- Do: Consider what they know about their couples' booking decisions and what they would like to ask a newly booked couple. No public engagement or offer ask.
