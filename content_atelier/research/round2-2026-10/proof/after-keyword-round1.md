**body:** Our team had brought the existing keywords and some proposed additions to the conversation. The team agreed to look into her suggestion and share any additional terms they found.

It was a small exchange, but it illustrates something we value in an advertising partnership. Our team contributed the recommendations and the commitment to research. The client contributed context about how people talk about her market.

**Your firsthand knowledge of couples deserves a place alongside our advertising expertise.**

When you're already investing in Google Ads, you want the people supporting you to bring considered recommendations. You also have knowledge built through the conversations you're having with people who might choose your venue.

Those contributions can work together. The person speaking with couples can offer context that's useful to the team researching how to reach them.

We believe a recommendation should give us something meaningful to discuss with you. There should be room for your experience to contribute while we're considering the strategy, with our team taking responsibility for investigating the questions that arise.

**ending:** If you've ever wondered where your own read on couples fits once you hire help with Google Ads, this is how we'd want it to work: our research in front of you, and room for what you know.

Talk soon,
Whitney

**script:** When a venue already has Google Ads running, what happens to the keywords that are in there?

That was the situation with one of our venue clients. Her ads were already running when our team sat down with her Google keywords. So the first thing they did was make a note of the current terms in the account. And our team thought those terms were good.

So the recommendations were built around them. The new keywords were meant to level up what was already in there, and the team kept the total focused, probably somewhere around 10 to 15 keywords.

Then they put the whole list in front of her, the current terms and the new ones side by side, and asked how it looked.

When you bring a new team into your Google Ads, the work already in your account doesn't have to be thrown out. I think a careful review starts with what's there, and then decides what to add on purpose.
