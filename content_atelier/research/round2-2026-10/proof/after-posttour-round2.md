**body:** She told me she pays attention to whether her venue is the first stop on a couple's tour list or the last one.

A couple fresh off their first tour is in a different place. They're still comparing, and they may not be ready to answer that.

That conversation is a good picture of how we think about follow-up. You're already generating inquiries, hosting tours and talking with couples yourself.
