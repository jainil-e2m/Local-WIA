**opening:** Hi {{contact.first_name}},

I was talking through post-tour follow-up with one of our venue clients recently, and I asked her what couples are usually ready to do when they walk out the door. Are they heading off to see more venues, or are they close to making a decision?

**body:** She told me she pays attention to one thing during a tour: whether her venue is the first stop on the couple's list or the final one.

When she knows she was the final stop, she follows up within the week and puts the question plainly but kindly. Have they chosen a venue, or are they still undecided? It's a fair question for a couple who has finished looking, and their answer helps her know how to proceed.

A couple fresh off their first tour is somewhere else entirely. They're still comparing, and asking them to decide would come too early.

**A tour tells you a couple came to visit, but what you learn during it tells you how close they are to choosing.**

She already had automated follow-up emails in place, and she was reaching out personally on top of them. She'd been wondering about adding texts after the tour as well, and I agreed we could incorporate them.

What stayed with me from that conversation is how much good follow-up depends on something the venue already knows. You're the one walking couples through the space. You hear whether they have other visits coming up or whether yours was the last one they planned. When we shape the emails and texts that go out after a tour, that's the knowledge we want them to rest on, so a couple who's still comparing and a couple who's ready to decide each hear something that fits.
