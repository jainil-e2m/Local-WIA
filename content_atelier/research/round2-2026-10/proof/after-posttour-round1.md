**title:** The week after a final tour

**script:** How soon should you follow up after a couple's last venue tour?

One of our venue clients walked me through how she handles this, and I really liked her reasoning.

When she knows her venue was the final stop on a couple's list, she checks in within a week. She asks them, nicely and properly, whether they've gone with a different venue or they're still undecided.

She also told me that at her venue, when hers is the last tour, couples usually know within the next few days, more often than not.

She also mentioned that follow-up for those last-tour couples might need to be customized a little, because they're in a different spot than a couple who's just starting to look.

That's the kind of thing we listen for when we help shape a venue's follow-up. How quickly her couples decide is something she learned from her own tours, and it tells us a lot about when a message should go out and what it should ask.
