## Rules for choosing a CTA

1. **Fit the channel.** DM keywords belong on Instagram. Email uses a reply keyword or an open reply question: "Why would we ask for a DM via email? Come on" (A-social-2, 2025-12-27_694f3d51). Threads posts carry no CTA (A-social-2, 2025-12-26_694ecc87).
2. **Not every piece sells.** A week mixes no-CTA pieces with one CTA piece. On 27 Jul 2026 she ran "2 clips without CTA and the thirds case study clip with CTA" (A-social-3, 2026-07-28_6a68eccd). "Perhaps this one should be nurture content, no CTA" (D-delivery-1, 2025-09-08_68befe84).
3. **One CTA per asset, matched to its job.** "a lead magnet, not a price list" (B-strategy-2, 2026-01-05_695b3024). A CTA must be a real next step: "Lol I wouldn't consider that a CTA at all" (A-social-3, 2026-04-06).
4. **A low barrier beats choosing an offer.** She preferred a problem-led reply CTA over asking readers to pick Accelerators or ELEVATE: "It was lower barrier, no?" (D-delivery-2, 2026-06-10).
5. **The sign-off follows the CTA.** If they must DM the brand, don't sign "XO Whitney" (A-social-2, 2025-12-29).
6. **Honest framing.** "no pressure, no pitch… is disingenuous given that I've already mentioned an offer" (A-social-2, 2025-12-26). No high-pressure claims (C-email-1, 2025-08-12).
7. **Check offer status and capacity first.** Elevate had no capacity in January 2026 (B-strategy-1); scarcity must be true (A-social-1, 27 Dec 2025).
8. **Pricing stays gated.** The sales page and prices come after contact capture "from a DM, application or investment guide opt-in" (A-social-1, 2025-12-26).
9. **Earn the offer.** Education first, the offer as the solution, then the CTA: "take people from cold to sold" (B-strategy-2, 2026-05-11_6a026ad2).
