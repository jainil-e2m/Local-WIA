- offer and sales assets (4);
- a borrowed format: a Threads template or an old launch carousel used as a "formula" (5);

## 3. A typical week

Example: the week of 27 Jul 2026 (A-social-3, 2026-07-28_6a68eccd, team-operated, Whitney's edits).

- **Inputs:** two podcast clips and one case-study talking head (transcripts), the voice guide, cover images.
- **Plan:**
  - "Were doing 3 posts total, use marketing discernment to choose when to use and what to use for CTAs."
  - Then: "2 clips without CTA and the thirds case study clip with CTA."
  - Covers planned as a set ("Control → Understanding → Confidence").
- **Output:** a cover title and caption for each clip. The case-study Reel and a matching email went out on Friday 31 Jul.
- **Next week (3 Aug):** "They did well." So the case study was retold as a nine-slide carousel, with its own caption and an email rewritten from the caption (A-social-3, 2026-08-04_6a721147).

**Volume in practice:**
- One list email a week ("send at least one a week", C-email-1, 2025-11-20).
- WIA: one email and two posts a week; LeadLock: one email a week (C-email-1, 2026-01-22).
- Weeks in mid-2026 show two to four Instagram pieces and one email (A-social-3).
- An April 2026 target was much higher: three TikToks a day, and one Instagram post plus a Story sequence and one Threads post a day (C-email-2, 2026-04-14). Nothing in the export shows it being met. **Confirm with WIA.**

**Timing.**
- Emails go out on Tuesdays: "Tuesday also happens to be when our emails usually perform best" (A-social-2, 2026-03-26).
- From recording to final caption took about 2.5 weeks for the September 2026 UVP pieces (A-social-3).

**Rules that run through every campaign** (see `rejection-rules.md`):
- one hook per format;
- sibling pieces "aligned without being redundant" (A-social-3, 2026-10-01);
- emails stand alone;
- not every piece carries a CTA.

## 5. Reuse is gated by performance

- A piece is repurposed when it did well. "They did well" triggered the carousel retelling (A-social-3, 2026-08-04).
- Old campaigns are updated rather than rewritten from scratch: "We're repurposing a lot of content from last year" for the audit giveaway (A-social-3, 2026-10-05).
- Old carousels are remade when they performed: a July 2025 carousel had "a good amount of profile visits and external link taps" and was remade in December 2025 (A-social-2, 2025-12-30/31).
- A caption that "always did a great job of driving investment guide downloads" was revised and reposted (B-strategy-2, 2026-01-05).

## 7. What Whitney brings to each brief

She pastes the following, so a Content Atelier brief should carry the equivalent:
- offer status, links and capacity;
- the approved sibling pieces and last week's posts;
- her own draft;
- client numbers and story details;
- market-research findings in her words;
- the framework (Savannah's) and the voice guide;
- the audience's temperature;
- and, for emails, send metadata (date, time, segment, subject, preview).

Sources: A-social-1 section 5; A-social-2 section 5; B-strategy-2 (05-11: "Her prompts already work as briefs"); C-email-1.

## 8. Suggested fields for `content_plan` (to confirm with WIA)

- What WIA is promoting this month and next, with each offer's status and capacity, and any launch or enrolment windows.
- Channels in scope: Instagram Reels, carousels, Stories and captions; Threads; email. TikTok and LinkedIn need confirming.
- Weekly volume and the CTA mix (for example, number of no-CTA pieces against CTA pieces).
- Email send day and segments.
- The week's anchor asset or assets (founder seeds), and pieces already published that week.
- Workshop and event dates (VAULT trainings, summits, the annual audit giveaway).
