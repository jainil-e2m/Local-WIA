## 1. How Whitney builds proof

Taken from her own case-study copy and her corrections: the $70K reel she recorded (B-strategy-3, 2026-07-07_6a4d4971), the posted $70K caption and carousel (A-social-3, 2026-08-04_6a721147), the 17-to-90 story (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-25_6a3d3a29), the 10-week story (A-social-3, 2026-08-05_6a73993f) and the 59-lead story (attachments V01, E15; C-email-2).

1. **Lead with the client's moment or the headline number, then tell the before.** "70K in new wedding bookings, all directly attributable to ads." Or the client's own words: "I couldn't do it all."; "It feels like our sales are on autopilot." Or a question the client asked themselves: "Have you ever looked at your ad account and thought, 'I honestly have no idea if any of this is working.'?"
2. **Give exact periods and a fair comparison.** "The first half of last year… more than $22,000 on ad spend and agency fees." She changed "The year before we met" to "The first half of last year" (A-social-3, posted 31 Jul 2026).
3. **Name the specific failure before WIA.** No conversion tracking; reports she couldn't read; "She had to hand-hold her agency"; another wedding ads agency, with sales that "couldn't actually attribute any sales to ads".
4. **Say what WIA did, as a system.** Google and Meta plus LeadLock nurture; tracking; landing page; "the right foundation in place".
5. **Use the client's own words** (true quotes only).
6. **Caveat honestly.** "And just to be clear, that's not the venue's total revenue for the year" (her paragraph in the 31 Jul 2026 email). Bookings from social, referrals, SEO and directories are excluded (B-strategy-3).
7. **Turn the story into a principle that serves the reader.** "A client story should never stay a client story" is in KB 02. Whitney does this herself: "The $70K is great, but honestly, that's not even the point" (A-social-3, 2026-08-04). She also refused narrow framings: "I don't want this to just speak to those with a second revenue stream" (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-25).
8. **Credit the foundation, not ads alone.** "Before his ads launched, he had the right foundation in place." (V01).
9. **Own the result.** "I don't want to skirt around that. I want to own it" (A-social-3, 2026-09-16). Never present it as an anomaly (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-29).
10. **The CTA is soft and fits the story's offer:** DM "ADS", DM "ELEVATE", or the waitlist.

## 2. Rules for proof (from her corrections)

- **Anonymise by default.** Use the client type and a market descriptor ("a venue marketing director in a saturated urban market", "one of our ELEVATE clients", "a wedding planning client"). "I don't want to use the clients names" (C-email-1, 2025-11-24). One client's name may not be used even where her numbers are public (B-strategy-3, 2026-07-16).
- **Use the right client type and offer.** Venue content uses venue results (B-strategy-1, 2025-10-02). A testimonial must come from the offer being sold (B-strategy-1, 2025-10-05).
- **Proof shows capability, never a prediction.** She won't send prospects other clients' numbers as a benchmark: "I wouldn't want to cherry-pick individual client results" (F-megaemail-1, 23 Sep 2026). "I like to use social proof to show early wins instead of promising" (C-email-1, 2026-02-16).
- **Never invent, composite or embellish** a testimonial, quote or number (rejection rule A01). In October 2025 ChatGPT fabricated attributed testimonials and a result for her; she caught it only because she didn't remember the feedback (B-strategy-1).
- **Exact story facts:** numbers, timing, emotion and causality ("she wasn't surprised. She was relieved", C-email-1, 2026-01-30).
- **Proof can backfire.** A venue client vetoed "1,200 weddings" because it read as too many weddings (D-delivery-2, 2026-09-18). Check how the client wants to be seen.
- **Every result needs an "as of" date.** Results move. By October 2026 one featured client was seeing a slowdown in leads (B-strategy-3).
- **Consent.** The GSA member contract (October 2025) lets WIA "share testimonials and results achieved" unless the member asks for them to be left out (B-strategy-1, 68efbcab). That covers Accelerator members only, and WIA should still confirm before naming anyone.
- **WIA's own numbers** are stated only as approved ("nearly doubled"), never as exact revenue (C-email-1, 2025-08-12).
