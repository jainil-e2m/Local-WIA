# Rejection rules (candidate for `rejection_rules`)

> DRAFT derived from Whitney Hodges' ChatGPT export (analysed 18 October 2026). Not confirmed by WIA; status: draft. Each item cites its source.

## How to read this file

- **Ban**: a hard rule. Generated angles, briefs or copy that break it are rejected or rewritten. A ban has a start date; copy written before that date (including Whitney's own) may break it.
- **Flag**: a rule Whitney enforces by judgement, or one her own approved copy sometimes breaks. A checker should warn and show the line, not reject.
- **Author**: who stated the rule in the export. "Whitney" means she typed it or it was relayed as her words. "Team" means a team member typing in her ChatGPT (Gisselle Jiménez, or the unnamed team member who ran the July to September 2026 social chats). Team rules are WIA house standards, not Whitney's verdicts.
- **Scope**: `angle` (what an idea is about and who it is for), `brief` (format, CTA, structure), `all`.
- **Count**: distinct correction events summed across the chunk reports; approximate.
- **Sources** use chunk report names (`A-social-1` … `F-megaemail-1`, `attachments`) plus a conversation file and date where they matter.
