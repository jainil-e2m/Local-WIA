# How we find booking opportunities in a venue's coming season

We keep upcoming dates in the ad strategy by finding a reason they're right for couples now, including a venue's ability to host a wedding without a long engagement.

## Central point

We keep a venue's coming season in view by connecting it to reasons couples could choose it, including the team's ability to support a shorter engagement.

## Strategic purpose and desired response

CONVERT · solution selling

Show the depth of ongoing advertising strategy as venue owners plan their next season. Use the shorter-engagement proposal to demonstrate how Whitney connects the owner's calendar with what the venue can offer couples, without introducing urgency or a seasonal promotion.

Desired response: I hadn't thought of my team's ability to move quickly as a reason couples could choose us. I want an advertising team that sees opportunities like that.

- Understand: A coming wedding season can be relevant to couples who want a shorter engagement when the venue can support that timeline.
- Believe: An advertising partner should connect the season an owner is planning with meaningful reasons couples could choose it.
- Do: Start a conversation about ongoing advertising support and the ELEVATE waitlist.

## Audience and reason to care

Venue owners already investing in advertising who are planning their next season's calendar and want their ads to reflect what their team can offer couples.

Their team's ability to move quickly may be a useful reason for couples to consider the coming season, rather than assuming every wedding requires a long engagement.

## WIA perspective

We want a venue's advertising to give couples a reason to choose the season ahead. When the team can bring a wedding together on a shorter timeline, that capability can be part of the invitation.
