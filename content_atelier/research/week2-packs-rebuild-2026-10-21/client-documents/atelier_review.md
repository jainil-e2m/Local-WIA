## Content Brief: Inquiry progression and attended tours
## Approved angle
## What we investigate when more inquiries are not consistently turning into attended tours.
## Central point: An advertising partnership should help a venue understand how inquiries progress and use that understanding to choose what to improve next.
## Strategic purpose
## CONVERT | Solution selling. Make ELEVATE's ongoing strategic work and LeadLock's inquiry visibility tangible through a real review. The desired response is: I want a team that can connect campaign activity with what is happening in my inquiry process and help me decide where to focus.
## The content should help a prospective client picture a working relationship: the team reviews recorded activity, listens to the owner and recommends a focused refinement. LeadLock is the supporting system; the team's interpretation is the service value being demonstrated.
## Audience and reason to care
## Venue owners receiving inquiries and tour bookings who are also seeing unresponsive couples, cancellations or missed tours. They may wonder whether lead quality, communication or scheduling deserves attention. This helps them understand what a useful review looks at before recommending changes.
## Acknowledge that growing inquiry volume is positive. Speak to an experienced owner who wants to understand the next opportunity, rather than assuming their entire marketing or sales process is failing.
## WIA perspective
## More inquiries and more tour bookings are meaningful progress. We also want to understand what happens between those steps. We review the inquiry journey, the follow-up and the owner's experience together so that our next recommendation responds to what we can actually observe.
## LeadLock gives us visibility into recorded inquiry activity and supports follow-up. Our expertise is in interpreting that information with the owner and choosing a sensible next step.
## The reasoning should remain visible: identify which step needs attention, consider the explanations supported by the conversation, and choose what to investigate or test. Several factors may contribute, and the owner's context helps us avoid a premature conclusion.
