# Free-model shakedown ledger (Phase N1, 23 October 2026)

Plan section 5.0c. Every failure seen while the whole pipeline ran on free OpenRouter models, on a scratch copy of the working
data, in the production images, is triaged here as one of three kinds:

- **(a) Pipeline defect**: the system crashed, hung, lost or duplicated work, wrote a wrong state, showed no reason or the wrong
  one, or broke a gate rule, whatever the model said. Reproduced offline and fixed at its cause with a test that fails before
  the fix; the fix applies to paid runs too.
- **(b) Handled as designed**: the model's answer, or an outside event, was bad, and the pipeline repaired it within its limits
  or failed visibly with the reason in the app. A pass: it proves a failure path the paid run relies on.
- **(c) Free-model artefact**: behaviour paid models do not show. Absorbed only in the free-model branch of the dispatch
  (`llm/routing.py`) or recorded and ignored, never fixed in the core.

Design defects of the screens (the visual check at desktop width, 1440 by 900) are listed with the pipeline defects: they are
the system's, not the model's.
