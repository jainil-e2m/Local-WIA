**What we investigate when more inquiries aren’t consistently turning into attended tours.**

An advertising partnership should help a venue understand how inquiries progress and use that understanding to choose what to improve next.

**CONVERT · Solution selling**

Venue owners receiving inquiries and tour bookings who are also experiencing unresponsive couples, cancellations or missed tours.

They want to understand whether lead quality, communication or scheduling deserves attention. This content shows what a useful investigation considers before recommending changes.

### 4. WIA perspective

More inquiries and more tour bookings are meaningful progress. We also want to understand what happens between those steps.

We review the inquiry journey, the follow-up and the owner’s experience together so our next recommendation responds to what we can actually observe.

LeadLock supports follow-up and gives us visibility into recorded inquiry activity. Our expertise lies in interpreting that information with the owner and choosing a sensible next step.

- **03:42–10:36:** Allie reports increased inquiries and tour bookings alongside ghosting. You and Fallon examine progression, attendance and follow-up opportunities.

- **CTA:** Invite a conversation about ELEVATE’s advertising and inquiry follow-up support.
