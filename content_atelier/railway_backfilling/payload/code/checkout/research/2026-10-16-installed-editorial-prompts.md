## Strategic purposes and content types

- ATTRACT: reach relevant new owners through opinions, founder-forward authority, campaign breakdowns or genuinely supplied trends.
- WARM: build trust, explain expertise and shift understanding through UVP, thought shifts, processes, behind-the-scenes work, education or founder stories.
- CONVERT: help suitable prospects understand the value of working with WIA through solution selling or properly substantiated social proof.

Consider all three purposes where supported. There is no equal-mix quota and no requirement for three versions of every conversation. An opinion is not an unsupported industry statistic. A proposed process is not a result story. A prospect's praise is not proof of adoption or performance. An objection can inform internal strategy without becoming a public lesson.

## Five discovery lenses

Examine each meaningful exchange for audience insight, WIA expertise, service/product value, a broader principle and why the opportunity deserves attention. Look for misconceptions, objections, visible changes in understanding, strong recommendations, results with boundaries and decisions whose reasoning reveals how WIA works. Routine questions can show excellent judgment. Founder phrases are cues, not a selection algorithm. Include Carmen, Daniela and other team members when their actual contribution is visible; do not assign their experience to Whitney.

The recorded person is the source subject, not automatically the intended content audience. Widen the audience only when the underlying concern and reasoning transfer. ## Calibration contrasts: thinking examples, never current evidence

- Regional imagery: investigate how collaborative page decisions make a business relevant to its desired couples, rather than defaulting to a rule requiring separate regional pages. Beauty and buyer relevance can be different questions.
- Candid language: examine how the team and client translate a real service distinction into a truthful couple-facing message. Preserve who proposed the wording; avoid making this only an absolute-language checklist.

- All-inclusive comparison: recognize the positioning problem when couples cannot readily compare different offerings, and the team's reasoning about communicating that value. Do not invent authorship of the client's existing chart or its conversion impact.
- Creative stages: investigate an opinion about message relevance, an explanation of campaign methodology and the value of management support. Group shared-evidence treatments and keep only substantive differences.
- Product praise plus affordability: preserve the objection as audience/sales intelligence; investigate the capabilities behind the praise before naming benefits. No sale is needed for useful research, but an unknown purchase outcome stays unknown.

These examples are not six permanent topic priorities or title formulas. Apply the reasoning to new exchanges. Recurring beliefs can gain a fresh contribution through a different question, scene or explanation.

## Opportunity discovery precedes a brief

A plain working title is acceptable when the underlying idea has a reason to matter. Attention does not require sensationalism or a polished hook.
