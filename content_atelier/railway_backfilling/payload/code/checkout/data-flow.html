Process updates across every phase

pipeline_runs · overall

Requested scope, frozen inputs, active/waiting/complete state and partial failures. A week may have several runs.

pipeline_jobs · live work

Stage and optional target, current step/message, measured units, attempt, heartbeat and lease. Parallel assets/briefs have independent jobs.

pipeline_events · history

Meaningful milestones, warnings, retries and failures. Heartbeats stay on jobs; approval authority stays on the content record.

Meaning discovers; references verify

Briefing always receives the chosen angle's original grounding. Hybrid passage search expands it; prior-angle search and reverse passage lineage recover previous coverage. Fuzzy matching helps locate spellings or spans, while exact originals and hashes establish provenance.

Brief implementation checkpoint

Five leased jobs prepare, research, contextualize, write and validate each brief. Four ordinary chat stages and at most three query-encoding batches are bounded and cached; optional gap/repair work is explicit. Current source and approval checks prevent stale review. Real content and runs are untouched; only the full-text index was added. Read implementation and verification status.

Saved context carries the difference forward

The brief keeps selected proof, qualifications, compact prior arguments/uses and known gaps. Assets consume that approved contract. Publication is confirmed separately; unavailable history or embeddings cannot imply “no similar content.”
