# Reviews and buying research answer different questions

Reviews can tell a business what clients thought of their experience; asking newly booked clients what influenced their choice can surface a different kind of evidence, potentially useful when shaping ads and landing pages.

Audience: Wedding-business owners who have customer reviews but want a better understanding of what influences couples’ decisions.

## Core insight

Praise about the experience does not necessarily explain the decision that preceded it. Asking about that decision may add information, but its answers and marketing value remain unestablished here [C3].

## Message spine

- Respect reviews; distinguish experience feedback from information about choosing the business [C3].
- Introduce asking newly booked clients about buying triggers as a proposed addition, not a replacement for reviews [C2–C3].
- Explain the proposed path from client-sent survey to shared responses to possible ad and landing-page messaging review [C2].
