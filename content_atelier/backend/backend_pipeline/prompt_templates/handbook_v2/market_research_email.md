When we onboard a new advertising client for ELEVATE, our full-service advertising management program, we spend a lot of time getting to know their wedding business.
We ask about their differentiators, services, and goals.
And typically, they provide great information. They know their ideal clients well.
But then we ask a question that almost always catches them off guard:
"Have you ever surveyed your clients to find out why they actually booked?"
Not asked for a review.
Not collected testimonials.
Actually asked their couples:
What mattered most when making this decision?
What almost stopped you from booking?
Who were you comparing us against?
Why did you choose us over other options?
And honestly?
I don't think we've ever received a "yes."
Which kind of blows my mind.
Wedding businesses are generally pretty good at asking for reviews.
But very few are collecting feedback about the buying decision itself.
This matters because most wedding businesses are making marketing decisions based on assumptions.
They think they know why clients hire them.
They think they know what their couples care most about.
But they've never actually collected the data, which means they don't have the full picture.

Decision-making style
What factors mattered most
The exact language they use to describe their problems and goals
