# Wedding Industry Ads Content Strategy
## Funnel Structure
### ATTRACT
Goal: Reach new wedding professionals and create awareness.
Pillars
- Polarizing Opinions
- Founder-Forward Marketing / Industry Authority
- Campaign Breakdowns
- Trends (approximately 5% of attract content)
### WARM
Goal: Build trust, demonstrate expertise, and shift beliefs.
Pillars
- UVP (Unique Value Proposition)
- Lightbulb Moments / Thought Shifts
- Unique Processes, Frameworks & Beliefs
- Behind-the-Scenes Process
- Education
- Founder Story
### CONVERT
Goal: Generate inquiries and sales conversations.
Pillars
- Solution Selling
- Social Proof
Objection Handling and Urgency will be added later if needed.
# Content Framework
Every piece of content should follow:
### Hook
(Content idea / opening statement)
### Problems, Desires, Symptoms
(Paint the current reality they're experiencing)
### Main Point
(The lightbulb moment you want them to have, lesson, education, or reframe)
### Transformation
(What happens when they solve the problem)
### CTA
Attract & Warm
- Follow
- Comment
- Engage [Ruling R-9: not every post sells; ATTRACT has no CTA, or follow and save; WARM a soft, low-barrier invitation (reply, a DM keyword, save) or none; CONVERT the offer's CTA from the CTA catalogue]
Convert
- DM
- Inquire
- Book a call

- With paid ads, you can target your dream couples specifically.

- If you are established, you are typically taking advice from people who are behind you.

- Virality is never the goal. Sales actually are. Booked couples. Booked shoots, booked weddings. Here’s the strategy that gets you that.

# CONVERT CONTENT
## Pillar 1: Solution Selling
### Content Ideas (Verbatim)
- You ran ads, watched the money vanish and turned them off.
- Telling yourself “ads just don't work for my business.”
- They do.
- You just didn't have a system.
- Talk about how to create that system
- Avoid wasted spend, lost confidence
- Launch correctly and actually book. 
- If you were fully booked in October and crickets in January, it's not a dead season.
- It's not a marketing problem.
- It's that you do not have an actual system for your ad strategy.
- And you have an “always on” ad system that fills the calendar regardless of season or algorithm
- Wedding pros obsess over seasonality and say ‘oh it’s just “dead season”..’ - It’s not. People who have actually mastered their strategy are booking out for 2028 right now. And that could actually be them if they have the right strategy/ads system working for them.
- Your ad leads aren't worse than your referrals.
- They're just colder.
- And if no one taught you how to warm them up, you can’t just keep blaming the ads for your follow-up problem. 
- If you think you're not tech-savvy enough to run ads, you actually don't need to be.
- It’s not necessarily about tech, that part is just pushing the right buttons - but thinking like a strategist is the hard part / that’s the skill. We teach both.
