## Scope

- WIA is an ads agency (Google and Meta). It does not run all of a client's marketing, and ads add to referrals, SEO and organic, which content never disparages (R-6; Whitney).
- LeadLock supports follow-up. It does not replace the owner's follow-up, it handles no contracts or invoices, and it cannot book clients automatically (C-email-2, 2026-04-07; B-strategy-2, 2025-12-08; Whitney).
- Automation is not AI conversation. LeadLock sends automated nurture emails and texts; no AI talks to couples (D-delivery-2, 2026-07-09; Whitney).
- There is no waitlist application. A buyer joins the ELEVATE waitlist (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-29; Whitney).
