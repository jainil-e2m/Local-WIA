These are patterns from WIA's finals, not formulas. Whitney rejects copy that feels "cookie cutter" (A-social-2, 2026-04-01, Whitney).

## Rules across formats
- **Openings are distinct across assets.** Each asset has its own hook and job. Siblings repeat the idea, never the language (A-social-1, 2025-11-18; B-strategy-2, 2026-05-11; Whitney).
- **Each CTA fits its channel** (`10-cta-catalogue.md`): DM keyword on Instagram, reply in email, none on Threads. CTAs vary across assets unless a person chose one (R-9; standard 2.4a e).
- **No hashtags. Minimal emojis** or none (A-social-2, 2026-01-09; Whitney and team).
- **Who speaks:** "we" for WIA; "I" and a personal sign-off only for Whitney's own experience. The sign-off follows whoever the CTA names (A-social-2, 2025-12-29, Whitney).
- **Time-bound details** (spots, deadlines) stay off designed assets so they remain evergreen (A-social-1, 2025-11-18, Whitney).
- **Channel set** (R-10, R-11): Reel, carousel, caption, email; Stories and Threads optional; no TikTok or LinkedIn.

## Reel
- **Job:** one supported claim. Up to three Reels per brief, each a distinct claim, fewer when evidence is thin (plan 4.5). The September UVP training became one email and three Reels (A-social-3, 2026-09, Whitney).
- **Source, one of two:**
  - **Existing clip:** cut word for word, with exact start and end lines verified against the transcript. Never rewrite or re-record: "It is what it is." Captions match the clip. Prefer longer clips to very short ones (A-social-2, 2026-03-29, Whitney).
  - **New script:** beats are (1) hook: a question, a result or a client conversation; (2) backstory in plain speech; (3) what WIA did or saw; (4) the client's own words; (5) the principle it proves; (6) a soft CTA or none (A-social-3, 2026-08-05 and 2026-09-23; B-strategy-3, 2026-07-02; Whitney).
- **Spoken opening:** the first line, distinct from the cover and caption. For B-roll with on-screen text the hook carries everything (B-strategy-2, 2026-03-23, Whitney).
- **Cover text:** a title, optional subhead. A week's covers are planned as a set with varied structures; don't start most with "Why"; a headline number can sit in the subhead (A-social-2, 2025-12-26; A-social-3, 2026-07-28, 2026-08-04, 2026-10-05; Whitney).
- **Read time:** state the spoken length of the script or clip.

## Carousel
- **7 to 10 slides** by default; WIA approved 11 and 12 as exceptions (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-10; A-social-3, 2026-08; Whitney).
- **A job for every slide**, with real text on each. No filler or "fluffy transition slide", and no phrase repeated across slides (B-strategy-2, 2026-03-23; A-social-3, 2026-08-04; A-social-1, 2025-12-27; Whitney).
- **Slide 1** hook differs from the Reel's and the email's. Dates on an evergreen carousel go on the second-to-last slide or in the caption (A-social-2, 2026-02-05, Whitney).
- **Seen patterns:** one client story told slide by slide; a perspective shift; an evergreen thought piece with no offer (A-social-2, 2025-12-31; A-social-3, 2026-08; WIA published).

## Email (list broadcast)
- **Header:** date, send time (Tuesday performs best), segment, subject options, preview text (C-email-1; A-social-2, 2026-03-26; Whitney).
- **Subject:** specific and observational, one she would open, such as "The question we ask every new client" (WIA published).
- **Body:** "Hi {{contact.first_name}}," then the opening scene or observation; the argument in short, flowing paragraphs; proof or a client story; the transformation, including changed thinking; the offer, if any, as the solution; the ending with its reply CTA (C-email-1, C-email-2, Whitney; standard 2.4a c).
- **Stands alone.** No Instagram reference and no assumed context: the list includes Google and ChatGPT leads who never see Instagram (A-social-2, 2026-01; A-social-3, 2026-08-04; Whitney).
- **CTA is a reply**, never a DM (A-social-2, 2025-12-27, Whitney).
- **Sign-off:** "Talk soon, Whitney"; "xo, Whitney" for personal stories; "Daniela & Whitney" for launches, names alphabetical (C-email-1, 2026-01-29, Whitney).

## Caption
- **Framework** (Savannah's, mandatory from 25 Jun 2026): Hook, Problems/Desires/Symptoms, Main Point, Transformation, CTA. The offer may appear as the solution before the CTA (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-25; A-social-3, 2026-09-15; Whitney).
- **Hook** differs from the Reel's spoken and on-screen hook and never repeats the cover text (B-strategy-3, 2026-06-04, Whitney).
- **What it adds:** the story, proof and principle the asset cannot carry. Approved captions mostly run 150 to 300 words (A-social-3, 2026, WIA published).
- **Ending:** the CTA for the piece's pillar, or none. "-Whitney" only on first-person captions (A-social-2, 2026-01-14, Whitney).

## Stories
A few text slides leading to the Reel, then the Reel itself; never near-copies of yesterday's (A-social-3, 2026-05-04 and 05-06, Whitney). Each Accelerator's evergreen Highlight ends "DM us to apply" (ELEVATE has a waitlist, no application) (A-social-1, 2025-12-27, Whitney).

## Threads
A short take within the 500-character limit, no CTA, used as a test bed and expanded later if it lands (A-social-1, 2025-10 to 11; A-social-2, 2025-12-26; Whitney).
