## Part A: Whitney's published Reels

**A1. Messaging before ads (September 2026).** "If I can't tell from looking at your website or Instagram why couples should choose to book you for their wedding day, then we don't have any business running your ads, at least not yet. [...] You can choose the right keywords. You can get the targeting right. You can build a technically sound campaign. But if after people click your ad, they're just seeing generic copy that doesn't set you apart from your competitors [...], your advertising results are going to fall flat. [...] That's why the work with our clients doesn't start in their ad accounts. It begins with working on things like messaging, positioning, and defining your unique value proposition. [...] When we know exactly who to reach, what matters to them, and why they should choose your business, we can carry that strategy through the entire campaign, from the keywords and targeting to the ad copy, landing page, and follow-up. [...] DM me "ads" and let's talk about what working together could look like."
The move: shows where WIA's work starts (positioning and value proposition), then one strategy carried through every piece. Her recorded hook replaced the AI's "If I don't know why someone should choose your business, I have no business running ads for it": concrete, and softened with "at least not yet".

The move: that week's member training becomes an email and Reels the same week, showing the depth of working with WIA instead of re-teaching basics.

## Part B: contrastive pairs and approved finals

**B1. Caption, 1 May 2026.** Draft: "Organic social right now? It's unpredictable at best… invisible at worst. [...] you're building on borrowed ground." Her final (em dashes removed): "Most of the visibility you're getting? It's coming from other vendors, past clients, friends, family. People who already know you… or were never going to book you in the first place. [...] They stop relying on organic to carry their marketing… And start amplifying it with digital ads." Why: "I'd never talk like that"; name the wrong visibility, be wedding-specific.

**B2. Caption, 12 May 2026.** Draft quote: "I just want to know this strategy will actually work." Her final: "One thing we hear often from wedding pros before they work with us is: "I don't want my ads to feel generic." "I want to know the leads coming in are actually aligned."" Why: nobody had said the draft's line to her; use what prospects really ask.

**B3. Caption, 30 April 2026.** Draft: "pre-consultation nurturing". Her final: "pre-consultation reminders and nurturing to pre-sell every appointment". Why: "I'm not sure a lead would really know what this means."

**B4. Email, 15 September 2026.** Draft: "Before we can write an ad that makes someone stop scrolling, we need to know why they should care." Her final: "Before you can write an ad that makes someone stop scrolling, you need to be able to articulate why they should care." Why: in the Accelerators the owner writes the ads with WIA's guidance, so "you".

**B5. Email, 16 April 2026.** Draft: "fully supported from first inquiry through to booking". Her final: "built inside LeadLock to support your inquiry process from "We're interested..." to "We're ready to book!"" Why: LeadLock handles no contracts or invoices.

**B6. Email, 27 January 2026.** Draft: "I keep seeing incredibly talented planners struggle to clearly communicate their value [...]". Her final: "I created this session for planners who are ready to amplify what's already working in their business." Why: ads are for owners already succeeding; "focus on the positive, not create barriers".

**B7. Email, 30 January 2026.** Draft: "Meta warms the audience. Google captures them when intent peaks." Her final: "Google captures high-intent searches first. Meta then retargets them and drives conversions." Why: the order WIA actually sees.

**B8. Carousel slide, 29 December 2025 (posted 14 January 2026).** Draft: "the gap isn't effort or quality… …it's control." Approved: "For established wedding businesses who have already invested in their work… the challenge is often control." Why: struggling vendors often haven't put in the effort; speak to established ones.

**B9. Stories, 13 April 2026.** Draft: "We build the foundation, guide the strategy, and support your decisions". Her final (em dashes removed): "We teach you the strategy, launch your campaigns, and support you as you learn what's working, and how to make it better." Why: "We don't build the foundation... They build it."

**B10. Reel text hook, 1 May 2026.** "You can't book clients who never find you." Why: a text-only Reel needs one strong line; she took it from her own email.

**B11. Email subject, 25 January 2026.** "The most common follow-up problem I see". Why: "Those all sound so generic to me."

**B12. Threads, 22 October 2025.** "You know your ads are working when a potential client tells you they found you through an ad and that it was really good" (emoji removed). Why: a real client moment, tested on Threads, later expanded into a carousel, email and Reel.
