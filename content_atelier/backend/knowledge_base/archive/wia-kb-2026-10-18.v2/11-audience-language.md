## Who WIA serves
WIA's ideal client is an established, growth-minded wedding business that wants to double down on what already works. Ads amplify that business. This replaces the pain-led "feast or famine" and "inconsistent inquiries" framing (ruling R-8). The prospects' words below show beliefs to shift and must never become a pain-led hook.

## Signals (Whitney)
- "Our ideal client is already running a thriving business, but they are ready to uplevel. They need ads to grow. And they know it." (B-strategy-1, 17 Oct 2025)
- "already successful wedding pros who really want to lean in on what's working and double down" (B-strategy-2, 2 Apr 2026)
- "Making sales, already seeing growth, putting real effort into organic marketing, knows they need to spend money to make money." (C-email-1, 16 Feb 2026)
- Strong brand: knows its ideal client, has a brand designer, a conversion-focused website, inquiries in Instagram DMs and organic Google leads (A-social-1, 23 Dec 2025).
- Valued most by those who "tried to DIY it and gotten stuck or who have worked with another agency or freelancer" (B-strategy-1, 17 Oct 2025).
- Keep the door open: "I don't want people to not apply because they are second guessing if their branding is strong enough" (A-social-2, 3 Feb 2026).

## Anti-signals (Whitney)
- "If they are wondering where their next lead is coming from, this definitely isn't for them" (A-social-2, 30 Dec 2025)
- "Our programs are NOT for people who are struggling" (C-email-1, 16 Feb 2026)
- On leaning into inconsistent inquiries: "IMO that speaks to desperate wedding pros" (B-strategy-2, 2 Apr 2026)
- Poor websites, and "wedding pros that are never showing up" (A-social-3, 13 Aug and 1 Oct 2026)
- "If they are looking for a DFY agency, and all they want is Meta Ads, we aren't a fit" (E-sales-1, 14 Jul 2026)

## Objections and Whitney's answers
- "Do I want to learn this? Do I even have the time?" The Google Search Accelerator builds the campaign for them (A-social-1, 27 Dec 2025).
- "Show me another client's numbers": a demo shows "how the system works", and she won't "cherry-pick individual client results" (E-sales-1, 25 Sep 2026).
- LeadLock lock-in: the client keeps its data, strategy and copy (E-sales-1, 29 Sep 2026).
- Referrals: "That's a great foundation to have. We love referrals." (E-sales-1, 4 Aug 2026)
- From her sales calls: creative quality; responsiveness after bad agencies; the Accelerators' time demand; doubt that ads work in their niche; ads versus SEO, a rebrand or a website (B-strategy-2, 23 Apr 2026).
