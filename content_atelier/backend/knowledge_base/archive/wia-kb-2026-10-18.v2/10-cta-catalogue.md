## Rules
- **The pillar decides whether to ask** (R-9). ATTRACT: none, or follow and save. WARM: a soft invitation below, or none. CONVERT: the offer's CTA below. In a week of three Instagram pieces, one carries an offer CTA.
- **The channel decides the form.** Instagram: a DM keyword. Email: a reply, never a DM ("Why would we ask for a DM via email?", A-social-2, 2025-12-27, Whitney). Threads: none (A-social-2, 2025-12-26, Whitney).
- **Vary CTAs across a brief's assets** unless a person chose one (standard 2.4a e).
- **A low barrier wins:** one reply beats picking an offer (D-delivery-2, 2026-06-10, Whitney).
- **Time-limited offers** (spots, deadlines) go in the caption only, never on the asset (A-social-1, 2025-11-18, Whitney). State scarcity only when WIA announced it that week (R 2.2).
- **No prices** in any CTA (R 2.2). The sign-off follows the CTA: a DM to the brand is not signed by Whitney (A-social-2, 2025-12-29, Whitney).
