## Week, volume and channels (rulings R-9 to R-11)

- One list email a week, sent Tuesday ("when our emails usually perform best", Whitney, A-social-2, 2026-03-26), and three Instagram pieces. Threads is optional. TikTok is out until WIA restarts it.
- Brief channels: Instagram Reel, carousel, caption, email. Threads and Stories optional.
- The strategist proposes as many cards as the week's evidence supports; WIA develops about three a week.
- Over a month: mostly WARM (proof of how WIA thinks and works), ATTRACT for reach, about one CONVERT piece a week.

## How a set is built

- **One real asset, many formats.** A set starts from something real: a published piece, a client check-in, a sales call, a VAULT training, a founder recording, a podcast. Whitney's move is to ask what it shows about how WIA works: "I don't really want to regurgitate it all" (A-social-3, 2026-09-15). The set runs over about two weeks: the 31 Jul 2026 case-study Reel and email became a carousel, caption and email the next week; the September 2026 UVP pieces took about 2.5 weeks from recording to final caption.
- **Reuse only what performed.** "They did well" turned the case study into the carousel (A-social-3, 2026-08-04). A 2025 carousel with "a good amount of profile visits" was remade; the audit giveaway reused last year's posts.
- **One hook per format.** Reel, caption, carousel and email each get their own hook and job ("reel = story, email = reflective, carousel = perspective shift"). The caption never repeats the on-screen text; siblings are "aligned without being redundant" (Whitney, A-social-1; A-social-3, 2026-10-01).
- **Deliberate CTA posts.** Not every post sells. Of three Instagram pieces a week, one carries an offer CTA (the week of 27 Jul 2026: "2 clips without CTA and the thirds case study clip with CTA", team). ATTRACT: no CTA, or follow and save. WARM: a soft reply, DM keyword or save, or none. CONVERT: the offer's CTA. Instagram uses a DM keyword, email a reply, Threads nothing.
- **Emails stand alone.** The list includes leads from Google and ChatGPT who never see Instagram, so an email never refers to Instagram and never asks for a DM. Evergreen emails also join the nurture sequence. Drafts carry date, time, segment, subject and preview (Whitney, A-social-2; C-email-1).
- **Video is cut verbatim from WIA's own recordings.** "I'm not going to re-record… It is what it is." Give exact start and end lines; captions match the clip; longer clips are preferred (Whitney, A-social-2; A-social-3, 2026-07-28).
- **Threads is the test bed.** Short takes go up first and are expanded weeks later, as the 22 Oct 2025 Threads post became a carousel, caption, email and Reel by 18 Nov (A-social-1).

- Reel, then caption, email, carousel and offer caption (May 2026, B-strategy-2).
