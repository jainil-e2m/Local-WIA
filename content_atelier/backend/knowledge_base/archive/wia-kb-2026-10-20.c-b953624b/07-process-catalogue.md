## Diagnosis beyond the dashboard
- WIA sees lead volume in the ad platforms. It asks clients for consult and booking outcomes ("I want to uncover the why"), proposes tour-conversion tracking and UTMs, and segments spam by network, location setting, age and campaign type (D-delivery-1, 2025-09-23_68d2ec37, 2025-10-02_68dde15f).
- Data-optimisation method: find the bottleneck (click-through rate versus landing page), then revisit the ideal client, the USP, lead alignment and the messaging (A-social-2, 2026-03-26 transcript).
- Whitney believes "advertising often does two things at once: it brings in new leads, and it reveals areas where refinement can unlock stronger results." (D-delivery-1, 2025-11-17_6917ae8a).
- What this shows about WIA. When leads rise and bookings don't, WIA looks past the ads to follow-up, the landing page and the sales process.

## Google + Meta Strategy & Budget
- Google Search: captures people already searching; site links, conversion tracking and conversion copy; the objective is leads.
- Meta Phase 1: a cold Client Attraction video campaign (example: $10 a day), excluding social engagers, the email list and website visitors, to grow retargeting audiences cheaply and test which messages resonate.
- Meta Phase 2: Attraction drops (example: $2 a day). A cold-plus-warm Conversion campaign without exclusions (testimonials, real-wedding footage, behind the scenes). Warm Retargeting (example: $1 a day) that overcomes objections, helps clients self-qualify, shares the process and answers FAQs.
- Whitney's 2025 description of the default plan (D-delivery-1, 2025-11-18): about $4 a day of cold video views or engagement, excluding warm audiences, "so we get in front of new people every day and creatives rarely fatigue"; conversion campaigns at $12 to $25 a day; warm retargeting at $1 to $5 a day; Google Search; email and text automations. "We pretty much don't ever run traffic campaigns to cold audiences."
- What this shows about WIA. Google and Meta play different roles: one captures intent, the other builds trust and retargets. Each campaign is judged by its own job. This is the concrete form of "Google + Meta work best together".

## The UVP method ("Why You?" VAULT workshop)
- Three questions. THE WEDDING (what the couple is trying to create), MY EDGE (what the business is particularly equipped to do), THE VALUE (why that matters to the couple). Workshop presenter document, 18 Aug 2026; the workshop ran in September 2026 (attachments D2; A-social-3).
- Tools. The "So What?" test: feature, then consequence, then experience, then emotional value. "Find Your Edge" prompts. A UVP Map (Couple, Vision, Edge, Value). A thinking sentence that is "NOT… your homepage headline". A UVP Analysis Prompt for ChatGPT (B-strategy-3, 2026-08-13; D-delivery-2, 09-15).
- Whitney's definition: "the clearest reason the right client should choose your business over the other options available to them" (B-strategy-3, 2026-08-13_6a7e2852).
- Whitney's framing for content: "I taught a training… that technically had nothing to do with running ads." The resulting email and reel showed "the depth of what working with us is like" (A-social-3, 2026-09-15_6aa98079).
- What this shows about WIA. WIA treats positioning as the foundation of ad performance, and its teaching goes beyond buttons and settings.
